Divina S58 · G80 M3 / G82 M4 — exhaust simulator

Physical synthesis: inline-6, firing order 1-5-3-6-2-4, twin-turbo, 7,200 rpm cut, propagating through a pipe network built from the CAD dimensions (downpipes, loop, 18° X-pipe, 650 mm J-pipes, muffler, Y, tips).

Spectrum (0–3 kHz). Fixed lines = pipe resonances; lines that rise with rpm = engine orders.
Length compensation The CAD has identical downpipes, so the +330 mm loop alone unbalances the banks (half-orders 1.5/4.5 appear). If the real car has a longer Bank 2 upstream, set the extra to ~330 mm.
EMULATION. This is not the S58: it fires 12 pulses per cycle (6 per revolution) through the same exhaust. The real S58 gives 3 per revolution.
1D waveguide model (c ≈ 420–620 m/s depending on temperature). Not a recording; validate with a dyno + microphone. CAD positions are placeholders, as stated in the technical guide.

Shift simulator
D1
Hold Space (or use the throttle) to pull away. With the gearbox on, rpm comes from speed, gear and torque converter, and the engine sound follows that rpm. The auto sweep becomes a launch with shifts. Locking the rpm turns the gearbox off. Paddles in D or S switch the gearbox to M.

RPM control
With the rpm locked the throttle stays at 25% or more, so there are no pops. Arrow keys ↑ and ↓ change 250 rpm and lock. When unlocked, the pedal and the sweep work as before.
